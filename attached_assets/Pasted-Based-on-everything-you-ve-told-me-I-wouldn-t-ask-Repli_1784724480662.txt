### One feature I'd add that most finance apps miss

A **"What If?" Budget Simulator**.

Users can move sliders like:

* Salary: ₹50,000 → ₹60,000
* Rent: ₹12,000 → ₹10,000
* Food: ₹6,000 → ₹5,000

…and instantly see how their **Safe to Spend**, **Savings**, and **Monthly Balance** change before making real decisions.

It's interactive, visually engaging, and a feature that helps your app stand out from typical expense trackers. It would make your template feel much more premium and memorable.
